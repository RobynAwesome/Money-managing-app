DFR-01 architecture

Perception and actuation stay separate.

01 / DRIVE + CHASSIS

Six-wheel rocker-bogie

Six Rhino IG52 24 V gearmotors, six 250 mm wheels and passive rocker-bogie suspension inside the 700 × 650 × 500 mm target envelope.

02 / COMPUTE + CONTROL

Jetson requests. Teensy decides.

Jetson Orin Nano Super handles perception. Teensy 4.1 validates bounded velocity commands and retains deterministic motor authority through three MDDS30 drivers.

03 / VISION + LINK

Local-first operation

RealSense D455 and RPLIDAR A2M12 feed local perception. Wi-Fi carries command/video; LoRa carries heartbeat and fail-stop only.

04 / POWER + SAFETY

Protected 24 V system

24 V 20 Ah LiFePO4, BMS, 60 A master fuse, contactor, emergency stop and separate logic rails. Loss of a valid command or heartbeat forces zero velocity and motor disable.

05 / TEST PROGRAMME

From movement to mission sequence

Loaded drive and turns → continuous onboard video → 30 cm bumps and 45° ramp → range and payload → mission objects → balloon sequence → autonomy, endurance and FMEA closure.
