DFR-01 architecture

PERCEPTION CAN ASK.
CONTROL DECIDES.

01 / DRIVE + CHASSIS

Six-wheel rocker-bogie

Six Rhino IG52 24 V gearmotors, 250 mm wheels and passive rocker-bogie suspension inside the target design envelope.

02 / COMPUTE + CONTROL

Jetson + Teensy

Jetson Orin Nano Super handles perception. Teensy 4.1 validates bounded velocity commands and retains deterministic motor authority.

03 / VISION + LINK

Local-first operation

RealSense D455 and RPLIDAR A2M12 feed local perception. Wi-Fi carries command/video; LoRa carries heartbeat and fail-stop signalling.

04 / POWER + SAFETY

Protected 24 V baseline

24 V 20 Ah LiFePO4, BMS, 60 A master fuse, contactor, emergency stop and isolated logic rails.

05 / TEST PROGRAMME

Movement → mission

Loaded drive and turns → continuous onboard video → 30 cm bumps and 45° ramp → range and payload → objects → balloon sequence → autonomy, endurance and FMEA closure.

CLOUD / LLM MOTOR AUTHORITY: NONE. Generative systems and cloud services do not sit in the motor actuation loop.
