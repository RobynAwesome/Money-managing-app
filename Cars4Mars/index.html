DRAG TO ORBIT · SCROLL TO ZOOM
01 / MOBILITY

Six-wheel drive

Six Rhino IG52 24 V gearmotors, 250 mm wheels and passive rocker-bogie suspension.

02 / CONTROL

Edge + deterministic control

Jetson Orin Nano Super handles perception. Teensy 4.1 retains motor and safety authority.

03 / VISION

Camera-first operation

RealSense D455 depth vision and RPLIDAR support local navigation and object work.

04 / COMMS

Local wireless

Wi-Fi carries command and video. LoRa is reserved for heartbeat and fail-stop signalling.

05 / POWER

480 Wh target

24 V 20 Ah LiFePO4, BMS, master fuse, contactor, emergency stop and isolated logic rails.

06 / ENVELOPE

700 × 650 × 500 mm

Target design envelope with a low central tray sized for the one-kilogram mission payload.
