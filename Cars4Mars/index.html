Rover architecture

AI can perceive. It cannot own motor authority.

DFR-01 separates perception from deterministic actuation. The Jetson may request movement; the Teensy validates bounded commands and retains motor and safety authority.

Drive & chassis

Six Rhino IG52 24 V gearmotors · six 250 mm wheels · passive rocker-bogie · 700 × 650 × 500 mm target envelope · 1 kg central payload tray.

Compute & control

Jetson Orin Nano Super for perception · Teensy 4.1 for deterministic motor control · three Cytron MDDS30 dual motor drivers.

Vision & communications

Intel RealSense D455 + RPLIDAR A2M12 · local Wi-Fi for command/video · LoRa for heartbeat/fail-stop only.

Power & safety

24 V 20 Ah LiFePO4 · BMS · 60 A master fuse · contactor · emergency stop · separate DC/DC logic rails.
